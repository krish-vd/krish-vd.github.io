02
Quantitative Finance · DCF Modelling

Reverse DCF Model Agent

A quantitative finance agent that inverts the traditional DCF model. Rather than projecting future cash flows to arrive at a price, this agent works backwards from a stock's current market price — solving for the implied growth rate via binary search over the DCF equation. Claude then interprets whether that implied growth is aggressive, conservative, or reasonable, and renders a margin-of-safety verdict. Built as part of my independent quant research practice.

ArchitectureComputation layer (binary search DCF solver) + Claude interpretation layer
InputsStock price · FCF · Shares outstanding · WACC · Terminal growth rate
OutputImplied growth rate (10-yr CAGR) · Valuation interpretation · Margin of safety

01
Graph TheorySearch
Brin & Page · Stanford · 1998

The PageRank Citation Ranking: Bringing Order to the Web

Conducted an independent research initiative inspired by this seminal paper. Engineered a miniature hyperlinked knowledge graph — a scaled-down Wikipedia prototype — modelling web pages as nodes and hyperlinks as directed edges in a stochastic adjacency matrix. Implemented the PageRank algorithm using iterative eigenvector decomposition to compute a stationary probability distribution, simulating a random surfer with a damping factor of 0.85. After running convergence cycles, visualised rank distribution across nodes — empirically confirming that nodes with higher indegree centrality and quality backlinks converge to significantly higher PageRank scores, mirroring how Google's infrastructure prioritises authoritative pages.

MapReduce: Simplified Data Processing on Large Clusters
